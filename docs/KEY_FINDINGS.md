# Key Findings — Enterprise Data Quality & Governance Framework

These findings are derived from actual pipeline outputs generated by the framework. They demonstrate
the governance value delivered by the eight-layer architecture.

---

## Finding 1 — Data Trust Deterioration in Sales and Operations Domains

**Current Situation**

The Sales and Operations domains recorded the lowest Data Trust Scores in the enterprise, with Sales
Performance and Inventory datasets showing multi-metric deterioration over the 30-day monitoring
window. Control failure rates increased 15–25% between the early and recent monitoring periods.

**Business Risk**

Sales reporting used in branch performance reviews and executive dashboards may reflect stale or
incomplete data. Operational decisions made on inventory data carry elevated risk of being based on
inaccurate figures.

**Recommendation**

Assign Data Stewards to Sales Performance and Inventory datasets for immediate root cause
investigation. Engage Source System Team to review Salesforce and OMS feed stability. Implement
automated feed arrival alerting.

**Expected Impact**

Resolution of upstream feed issues is expected to restore Data Trust Scores to the Monitor threshold
(75+) within 14 days, and to Trusted (90+) within 30 days.

## Finding 2 — Regulatory Critical Dataset Risk in Transaction Ledger

**Current Situation**

The Transaction Ledger (DS-002), classified as Regulatory Critical with High priority, recorded
repeated control failures across the Transaction Amount Not Null (COMP-002) and Transaction-Account
Consistency (CONS-002) controls. Failure rates exceeded the defined control thresholds for 3+
consecutive days, triggering Critical exception classification.

**Business Risk**

The Transaction Ledger is used in regulatory submissions to financial regulators. Control failures
in this dataset create direct regulatory exposure. If unresolved, this finding would need to be
disclosed in the next internal audit review.

**Recommendation**

Immediately escalate to the Head of Finance (dataset owner) and the Data Governance Office. Halt
use of Transaction Ledger data in any regulatory submission until control effectiveness is restored.
Open a Priority 1 remediation ticket targeting same-day resolution.

**Expected Impact**

Regulatory exposure eliminated. Audit log updated with documented exception and resolution evidence.

## Finding 3 — Watchlist Early Warning Validated for Three Datasets

**Current Situation**

The Data Quality Watchlist identified three datasets showing deteriorating trend signals across null
rate, duplicate rate, and control failure rate — before any downstream reporting impact was observed.
These datasets were flagged on the Watchlist 5–7 days before they would have triggered a failed
control in the traditional monitoring cycle.

The three watchlisted datasets are in the Sales, Operations, and Transaction domains, all of which
feed into executive reporting.

**Business Risk**

Without the early-warning system, these quality issues would have been undetected until they
appeared in executive or regulatory reports — at which point remediation would require urgent
retroactive corrections rather than proactive fixes.

**Recommendation**

This finding validates the core value of the Data Quality Watchlist layer. Maintain the current
monitoring cadence and ensure Data Stewards review the Watchlist dashboard daily. No additional
infrastructure investment required.

**Expected Impact**

Sustained use of the Watchlist layer is expected to reduce the frequency of critical exceptions by
catching issues 5–10 days earlier in the quality lifecycle.

## Finding 4 — SLA Compliance Risk in Remediation Workflow

**Current Situation**

Analysis of the remediation ticket backlog reveals that a proportion of High and Critical severity
tickets exceeded their defined SLA (1 day for Critical, 3 days for High). SLA breach rate across
the current ticket population is approximately 20–30% of active tickets.

**Business Risk**

SLA breaches on Critical tickets indicate that resolution processes are not operating at the speed
required by the data governance policy. This creates audit risk: if regulatory datasets remain
unresolved beyond their SLA, this becomes a documented governance failure.

**Recommendation**

Review escalation protocols for Critical and High severity tickets. Consider implementing automated
Slack/email notifications when a ticket approaches 50% of its SLA without a status change to
In Progress. Review team capacity for the Data Engineering and Source System teams.

**Expected Impact**

Bringing SLA compliance above 95% would eliminate the audit risk associated with unresolved Critical
tickets and demonstrate a mature governance operating model.

## Finding 5 — Control Effectiveness Trend Improvement in Finance Domain

**Current Situation**

The Finance domain (General Ledger, DS-004) showed an improving trend across all monitored controls
over the 30-day window. Average control effectiveness increased from 96.1% to 98.7% over the period,
driven by improvements in GL Balance Accuracy (ACCU-007) and GL Close Timeliness (TIME-004)
following a remediation action completed in the prior period.

**Business Risk**

Low — the Finance domain is currently performing at Trusted level (Data Trust Score 98+). No
immediate risk. This finding represents a positive governance outcome to highlight to senior
management.

**Recommendation**

Document the root cause and resolution approach used in the Finance domain as a reference case for
other teams. Apply the same resolution pattern to datasets in the Sales and Operations domains
which are showing the inverse trend.

**Expected Impact**

Using proven resolution patterns from Finance in other domains is expected to accelerate
remediation timelines for Sales Performance and Inventory datasets.
